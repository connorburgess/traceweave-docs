Traceweave separates research inputs from normative specifications ([research/README.md](https://github.com/connorburgess/traceweave-spec/blob/roadmap-v2/research/README.md)). External papers and generated reports are treated as hypothesis sources and audited claim-by-claim against primary sources before informing any design choice. Negative results are published alongside positive ones.
